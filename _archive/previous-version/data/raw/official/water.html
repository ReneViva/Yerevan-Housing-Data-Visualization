In 2026, the drinking water tariff for consumers will not increase

News listing
26 Nov. 2025

At the session held on November 26 of the current year, the Public Services Regulatory Commission of the Republic of Armenia set the 2026 tariffs for drinking water supply and wastewater (sewage treatment) services provided to subscribers by ‘Veolia Djur’ CJSC. For socially vulnerable subscribers, the 2026 tariff is set at 180 AMD, and for all other subscribers at 200.42 AMD. As a result of the monitoring carried out by the Commission, an additional volume of around 492,000 m³ was calculated, which had a positive impact on the tariff. In addition, the change in the volume of retail water supply also had a positive impact.
